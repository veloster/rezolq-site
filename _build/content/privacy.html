Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Effective: 8 November 2026 · Applies to the REZOLQ app for iOS and Android and to this website.

In short: REZOLQ has no accounts. During a show, the app and our show server use Bluetooth signals (and UWB on compatible phones) to work out where your screen is in the crowd; this data is linked only to a random show number, not to your identity, and is deleted within minutes. Your e-mail address is collected only if you choose to give it at the end of a show. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell data.

2. What the app does

REZOLQ turns the phones of an audience into a synchronised light display during an event. To show the right colour on your screen at the right moment, the app works out where your phone is in the crowd during the show.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Bluetooth (iOS: "Bluetooth"; Android: "Nearby devices") — used during the show to work out your place in the crowd. You can revoke it at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Location (Android) — Android treats Bluetooth placement as location, so the app asks for it. It is used only to work out your place in the venue while the app is open. REZOLQ never reads your GPS position.
  • Nearby Interaction / UWB (compatible iPhones and Android phones) — for more precise placement.
  • Camera (iPhone) — only to switch on the flashlight in time with the show. Nothing is filmed or recorded.
  • Network — to receive the show during the event.

5. Legal basis (EU/EEA users)

Where the GDPR applies: show data (show number, Bluetooth, UWB, place) is necessary to provide the show you choose to join (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); abuse protection and website and server logs (IP address) rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and available (Art. 6(1)(f)); your e-mail address relies on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in the organiser's or partner's e-mails, or by writing to us, without affecting what was done before.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers. E-mail addresses are passed only to the show's organiser and, if you ticked its box, to its partner. The show report given to the organiser contains only aggregate crowd statistics, never individual positions. Our show server is hosted by Fly.io, Inc. (USA), as our processor, on servers in Stockholm, Sweden; as Fly.io is a US company, its staff may access the servers from the United States. Fly.io states that it takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Fly.io Data Privacy Framework Privacy Policy). This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. in the United States, which receives the IP address of every visitor. GitHub states in its Privacy Statement that it takes part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and relies on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914) for transfers from the EEA. The website loads no other third-party resources: fonts are hosted on rezolq.com itself, and there are no cookies, analytics or advertising tags. Apple and Google may process crash and diagnostic data under their own privacy policies if you have enabled that on your device.

7. Retention

Bluetooth data is deleted after about 3 minutes and UWB data after about 10 minutes; your place in the crowd exists only during the show. Show numbers in the show report are kept until the organiser resets it. E-mail sign-ups are deleted automatically 1 year after sign-up. Server backups are kept for 5 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a portable format (data portability), to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, to withdraw your consent, and to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live, work or where the issue happened (in France: CNIL, cnil.fr; in Norway: Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no). If you signed up by e-mail, we can find, export or delete your sign-up; other data is linked only to a random show number and deletes itself within minutes. Write to contact@rezolq.com and we will answer within 30 days.

9. Account deletion

REZOLQ has no accounts, so there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. To stop receiving e-mails, use the unsubscribe link in any e-mail you receive. To have your sign-up deleted from our server, write to contact@rezolq.com.

10. Children

REZOLQ is not directed at children under 13 (or the age set by local law) and does not knowingly ask them for personal data.

11. Security

Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The Bluetooth broadcast contains only the random show number and is not encrypted.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app starts processing new categories of data, we will update this page, change the date at the top, and update the privacy details shown on the App Store and Google Play before the change takes effect.
