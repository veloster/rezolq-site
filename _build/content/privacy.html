Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Effective: 7 November 2026 · Applies to the REZOLQ app for iOS and Android and to this website.

In short: REZOLQ does not ask you to create an account and collects no personal information about you, apart from technical connection logs (IP address and time) kept for 30 days at most. During a show, the app uses Bluetooth to place your screen in the picture. That happens on your phone. That processing happens on your phone. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell data.

2. What the app does

REZOLQ turns the phones of an audience into a synchronised light display during an event. To show the right colour on your screen at the right moment, the app uses Bluetooth at the venue during the show.

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise GPS location, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers, or any payment information.

Is providing data required? No. You never have to give us personal information to use REZOLQ. Granting Bluetooth access is voluntary; without it the app cannot place you in the crowd and only shows a solid colour.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Bluetooth (iOS: "Bluetooth"; Android: "Nearby devices") — needed during the show so your screen shows the right colour at the right place. On iPhone the app does not ask for Location access. You can revoke it at any time in your phone’s settings; the app will then only show a solid colour.
  • Location (Android only, older versions) — on some Android versions the system requires Location permission for any app that uses Bluetooth. REZOLQ does not read or store your GPS position.
  • Network — to receive effect cues during a show.

5. Legal basis (EU/EEA users)

Where the GDPR applies, we process the technical data above on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): running the light show you have chosen to take part in, and keeping this website and the cue server secure and available. You can ask us for our assessment of these interests at any time. Bluetooth scanning only starts after you grant the permission on your device.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers. Your position in the crowd never leaves your phone, so neither we nor the event organiser receive it. Our show server is hosted by Fly.io, Inc. in the United States, which processes connection data on our behalf. Fly.io states that it takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Fly.io Data Privacy Framework Privacy Policy), which covers transfers from the EEA. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. in the United States, which receives the IP address of every visitor. GitHub states in its Privacy Statement that it takes part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and relies on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914) for transfers from the EEA. The website loads no other third-party resources: fonts are hosted on rezolq.com itself, and there are no cookies, analytics or advertising tags. Apple and Google may process crash and diagnostic data under their own privacy policies if you have enabled that on your device.

7. Retention

Bluetooth signals and your position in the picture exist only while a show moment is running and are discarded afterwards. Technical connection logs (IP address and time) on our show server are deleted after 30 days at most. We keep no database of participants.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a portable format (data portability), to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live, work or where the issue happened (in France: CNIL, cnil.fr; in Norway: Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no). Because REZOLQ holds no personal data about you apart from technical logs kept for 30 days at most, there is usually nothing for us to retrieve or delete — but you can always write to contact@rezolq.com and we will answer within 30 days.

9. Account deletion

REZOLQ has no accounts, so there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.

10. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age set by local law).

11. Security

Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Apart from technical logs kept for 30 days at most, we store no personal data.
