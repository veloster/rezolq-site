Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Effective: 7 November 2026 · Applies to the REZOLQ app for iOS and Android and to this website.

2. What the app does

REZOLQ turns the phones of an audience into a synchronised light display during an event. To show the right colour on your screen at the right moment, the app needs to know roughly where you are standing in the venue. It does this by listening to Bluetooth Low Energy beacons installed by the event organiser.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Bluetooth (iOS: "Bluetooth"; Android: "Nearby devices") — required to detect the venue's beacons. The app cannot position you without it. REZOLQ beacons use standard Bluetooth Low Energy advertising, so on iPhone the app does not ask for Location access. You can revoke it at any time in your phone's settings; the app will then only show a solid colour.
  • Location (Android only, older versions) — on some Android versions the system requires location permission to scan for Bluetooth beacons. REZOLQ does not read or store your GPS position.
  • Network — to receive effect cues during a show.

5. Legal basis (EU/EEA users)

Where the GDPR applies, we process the technical data above on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): running the light show you have chosen to take part in, and keeping this website and the cue server secure and available. You can ask us for our assessment of these interests at any time. Bluetooth scanning only starts after you grant the permission on your device.

6. Sharing

GitHub states in its Privacy Statement that it takes part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914) for transfers from the EEA. The website loads no other third-party resources: fonts are hosted on rezolq.com itself, and there are no cookies, analytics or advertising tags. Apple and Google may process crash and diagnostic data under their own privacy policies if you have enabled that on your device.

7. Retention

Beacon signals and your estimated position exist only while an effect is running and are discarded afterwards. We keep no database of participants.

9. Account deletion

11. Security
