What makes it different

Nobody picks a section. The crowd maps itself.

Other crowd-light apps ask each person to tap the block they are sitting in. Get it wrong and the picture breaks. REZOLQ places professional Bluetooth beacons around the venue; the beacons measure their own distances with ultra-wideband, and every phone locates itself against them. The result is a precise map of the crowd — and pictures that actually look like pictures.

  • Automatic placement Bluetooth beacons, no manual input from the audience.
  • Real shapes Logos, text and animations readable from the stage and on camera.
  • Battery-friendly The app only runs during an effect, not for the whole evening.
  • Private by design No account, no tracking, no data sold. Bluetooth is used for positioning only.

For organisers & show designers

Six beacons. One field. The whole crowd.

A REZOLQ installation is deliberately light: a handful of waterproof beacons on stands around the pit, measured against each other automatically. No cameras pointed at the audience, no grid of hardware to maintain. Effects are designed alongside your stage visuals, so LED walls, totems and the crowd move as one picture.

Privacy at a glance

What the app uses, and what it never does.

The full text is in the Privacy Policy.

Uses

  • Bluetooth — to detect the venue's beacons and estimate where your phone is in the crowd. Processed on your phone.
  • Screen & brightness — to show colours during an effect.
  • Internet (during a show) — to receive the effect cues from the show controller.

Never

  • No account, no e-mail, no phone number, no name.
  • No camera, no microphone, no contacts, no GPS tracking.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no selling of data.

Questions people ask

Do I need to create an account?

No. Download the app and you are ready. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to delete afterwards.

Which phones work?

Any iPhone or Android phone with Bluetooth. Exact minimum OS versions are listed on each store page.

Why does it ask for Bluetooth?

Bluetooth is how your phone hears the beacons around the venue and works out its position. It is not used for anything else, and the signal never leaves your phone.

Does it drain my battery?

Only while an effect is running — typically a few minutes per show. You can close the app the rest of the time.

I'm an organiser. What do I need?

A REZOLQ beacon kit (up to six beacons) and a show controller. Contact us and we will walk through your venue together.

Something isn't working.

Check Bluetooth is on and the app has permission, then see the Support page or write to us. We answer within two working days.
