Why you’ll want it

Why download it

The best seat in the house is your phone.

At a REZOLQ show, the crowd is part of the production. Without the app, you watch it. With it, you’re on it.

You’re part of the picture

Your screen joins thousands of others to draw shapes, waves and images across the whole crowd. Without you, there’s a gap.

A moment you’ll want to film

Seen from the stage or from the stands, the crowd becomes one giant screen. It’s the shot everyone posts after the show.

Nothing to set up

Open the app when the show asks, raise your phone. That’s it — your screen knows what to do.

Free, no account, no ads

No sign-up, nothing sold. Your e-mail is only asked if you choose to sign up for news at the end of a show. The app only wakes up for the moment itself.

Three moves. That’s all.

Download REZOLQ

Questions

Questions people ask

Is REZOLQ free?

Yes. Download it, open it at the show, and you’re in.

Do I need an account?

No. No account, no password. Your e-mail is only asked if you choose to sign up for news at the end of a show.

When do I open the app?

Only when the show asks you to. The artist or the screens will give the signal — open REZOLQ, raise your phone, and you’re part of the picture.

Will it drain my battery?

Barely. The app only runs during the moment itself, usually a few minutes. Close it the rest of the time.

Do I need a connection?

Yes, mobile data or Wi-Fi during the show. Download the app before you arrive: networks get busy when thousands of people are in the same place.

Why does it ask for Bluetooth?

So your screen shows the right colour at the right place in the picture, and so the show still reaches you if the network is busy. More in our Privacy Policy.

I’m sensitive to flashing lights.

Some moments use flashing colours. Turn on gentle mode on the app’s start screen: flashes become soft changes and the flashlight stays off. If flashing lights affect you, also look away from the screens around you.

Something isn’t working.

See the Support page or write to us. We answer within two working days.
